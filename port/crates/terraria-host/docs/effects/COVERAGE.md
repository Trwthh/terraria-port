## Passes

Columns: `ps` / `pre` / `vs` = instruction counts of the pixel shader, its preshader and the vertex shader; `WGSL` = translated (the test `translate` also runs every module through naga's validator);
`linear` / `point` = largest error of the GPU against the interpreter in 8-bit steps over the pixels that are not on an edge, in the bilinear and the point-sampled run; `edges` = pixels above one step that sit on a discontinuity of the shader (see README.md); `colours` = distinct colours of the test image.
